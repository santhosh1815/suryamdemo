About

Marketing meets machine intelligence.

Hey, I'm Surya — based out of Bangalore. I spend most of my day helping founders and marketing teams figure out what's actually working, and quietly kill everything that isn't.

For the last four years I've worked across healthcare, real estate, retail, finance and D2C — running SEO, paid ads, websites, social, and lately a lot of AI automation. I'm less into buzzwords and more into building things that keep running after I log off: clean funnels, sharp positioning, and workflows that stop wasting your team's time.

I also run AKOON IGNITE and GetWy — two small bets on the idea that good marketing should feel a little more human and a lot less like a template. If you're building something interesting, I'd love to hear about it.
